A FEW DETAILS

Built for your workflow.

Read the documentation
Do I keep using Claude Code and Codex?

Yes. Dockingbird runs the tools you already use, each in its own container and private workspace. You can work in a terminal or give an agent a background task and follow its reports through the bus.

Can I bring my own instructions and tools?

Yes. Configurations can carry role instructions, skills, model settings, environment variables, and limits. Your project’s mise toolchain and trusted repository settings shape the environment. Hook support depends on the harness.

Where can the agents run?

Dockingbird supports Apple silicon Macs and arm64 Linux. Start on one machine, connect another host, or set up a fleet with a shared queue. The documentation covers the setup and prerequisites for each.

What happens when I join the waitlist?

We’ll email you about Dockingbird early access and availability. There’s no payment or commitment to join. You can ask us to remove your email at any time.
