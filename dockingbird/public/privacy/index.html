Privacy.

This notice covers the Dockingbird website and early-access waitlist.

What the form collects

When you join, we save your email address, the signup time, and a record of the signup consent and page source. We use your email to contact you about Dockingbird early access and product availability.

How the signup is processed

The website and waitlist database run on Cloudflare. Cloudflare Turnstile checks that a signup is legitimate. Verification data, including the request’s IP address, is sent to Cloudflare for this check. The waitlist database does not store your IP address or verification token.

Website analytics

We use Google Analytics, loaded through Google Tag Manager, to understand how visitors use this website. Analytics may use cookies and collect information about page visits and browser or device activity. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Your choices

Joining is optional. To leave the waitlist, request deletion, or ask about your signup, email alex@zeus.fyi from the address you signed up with.
