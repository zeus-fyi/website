Research examplesUS 8,842,454 B2

Electrical engineering · Power electronics

A solar-power claim. An earlier power-supply architecture.

Aggregate current and voltage sensing, with local controllers for individual inverters.

GridScale SolutionsUS 8,842,454 B2 · Claim 1Submitted research

The technical question

Where had a primary controller already used total AC current and voltage to coordinate multiple inverters, each with its own secondary controller?

The research approach

The research followed the control architecture across neighboring engineering fields. A 1998 power-supply patent describes parallel transverters, common output sensing and local switching control.

Cross-field research

Search the underlying engineering mechanism, then map each element back to the claim.

Also recorded in the research

The alternative grounds, kept visible.

1 backup ground beside the submitted chart

Each alternative ground below was researched and recorded with the submission. Its verdict is the one recorded in the research; alternatives are not scored on the band scale unless the record scores them.

Backup ground for 1a · Obviousness over US 5,764,503 A in view of US 2010/0057267 A1 (General Electric)

References

US 5,764,503 A (Brand et al., Digital Equipment Corporation) · US 2010/0057267 A1 (Liu, Garces and Bose, General Electric)

Recorded verdict

Filed with the submission on 9 October 2026 as the backup ground for limitation 1a, for the reading in which a respective DC power source must be an energy source such as a photovoltaic array. The re-score of 19 October 2026 rates this ground C, hold 62, its weakest limitation 1a at C 71; it stands below the anticipation unless that reading is adopted.

Both references coordinate several paralleled inverters on one AC output through a central controller fed by voltage and current sensing of the common output, with a local controller at each inverter. Substituting photovoltaic arrays for the utility-fed regulators as the per-inverter DC sources is the use of a known technique on a known device with predictable results.

1a

A plurality of inverters configured to receive DC power from a respective DC power source and respectively provide AC power to an AC load.

“a plurality of PV subsystems 42, each subsystem having at least one PV array 12 and a PV inverter 10 operable to supply electrical power to a utility grid 14”

US 2010/0057267 A1 · [0032] · FIG. 2

The PV arrays 12 are the respective DC power sources of the paralleled inverters; US 5,764,503 A supplies every other limitation as charted above.

1a

The two-stage inverter of the GE reference.

“Typical PV inverters employ two stages for power processing with the first stage configured for providing a constant DC voltage and the second stage configured for converting the constant DC voltage to AC current.”

1b
