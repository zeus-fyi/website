Research examplesUS 8,842,454 B2

Electrical engineering · Power electronics

A solar-power claim. An earlier power-supply architecture.

Aggregate current and voltage sensing, with local controllers for individual inverters.

GridScale SolutionsUS 8,842,454 B2 · Claim 1Submitted research

The technical question

Where had a primary controller already used total AC current and voltage to coordinate multiple inverters, each with its own secondary controller?

The research approach

The research followed the control architecture across neighboring engineering fields. A 1998 power-supply patent describes parallel transverters, common output sensing and local switching control.

Cross-field research

Search the underlying engineering mechanism, then map each element back to the claim.

Source excerpt

US 5,764,503 A

Column 6, lines 55–60 · Figures 13–14

“Current voltage sensor 22 monitors the total voltage and total current being supplied by one (or more) transverters operating in a particular voltage phase.”

The mapping

For limitation 1b, the submission maps sensor 22 and system control module 18 to aggregate current and voltage feedback. Further quotations trace that feedback to the primary control signal.
