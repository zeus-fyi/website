Research examplesUS 9,331,488 B2

Electrical engineering · Power electronics

The operating state hidden in an archived manual.

Multi-input monitoring, LED indications and the distinction between a warning and an operating limit.

CyboEnergyUS 9,331,488 B2 · Claim 16Submitted research

The technical question

Which earlier inverter documents connect monitored input channels to the five claimed system-status indications?

The research approach

The research examined archived manufacturer manuals alongside patents and original-language documents. It separated a multi-LED reading from a ground combining the monitored architecture with single-indicator disclosures.

SUNWAYS · SOLAR INVERTER AT27

Source text · Typeset excerpt

Operating LED

The combined red/green LED indicates the device status of the Solar Inverter:

LED green, continuously lit Solar Inverter is active and feeds into power grid (MPP mode)

LED green, flashing Solar Inverter is active and feeds into power grid, however with current, output or temperature limitation.

LED red, continuously lit an error has occurred (malfunction)

LED red, flashing a warning has been issued

Highlighted text from the reference cited in the research record.

Recorded assessment: 15 October 2026. Scores assess the evidence for this ground; they are not probabilities of invalidity. P2 is the reason to combine, a claim-wide point.

The complete mapping uses several passages. The research record separately addresses the meaning of “input channel”.

Limitation 16b · A 97

SMA expressly connects the device’s operating mode with the integrated LEDs.

This passage supports actuation of the indicators. Whether the claimed states appear on one system-status LED is assessed separately.

Limitation 16c1 · B 75

Sunways describes one combined red/green LED, continuously green during grid feeding. The chart combines that indication with SMA’s monitored-input architecture.

B 75 is the recorded strength of the combination, including its reason to combine. This source passage is one part of that ground.

Limitation 16c2 · C 50 · Weakest

The manual describes continued grid feeding with a current, output or temperature limitation. Its separate red-flashing state states a warning, but does not say whether feeding continues.

This mapping depends on treating the operating limitation as a warning, the manual’s date evidence, and the reason to combine references. That uncertainty remains visible.

Sunways AT manual · §5.1 · Printed p. 27

Limitation 16c3 · B 75

The Sunways combined LED shows a continuously red error state. The chart also cites Fronius, Soladin and a Japanese reference for error indications.

The score includes the reason to combine these indications with SMA’s architecture. A single isolated passage is not the complete ground.

Sunways AT manual · §5.1 · Printed p. 27

Limitation 16c4 · B 75

The Sunways display distinguishes an AC-network error. The chart relies on Soladin and the Japanese 366 publication for a grid-specific code on one LED.

The highlighted Sunways text describes a display code, not a distinct grid-error LED state. The other references supply that part of the combination.

Sunways AT manual · §5.5 · Printed p. 38

Limitation 16c5 · B 75

Sunways identifies the unlit state as inactive night mode. The chart cites corresponding states in the Soladin and Fronius documents.

The recorded B 75 reflects the combined ground and its reason to combine, rather than this sentence alone.

Sunways AT manual · §5.1 · Printed p. 27

Claim-wide point P2 · B 78

The recorded rationale addresses putting SMA’s monitored operating states onto a single two-colour indicator. It is a claim-wide point, not an additional limitation.

The assessment records a three-reference combination. This point affects the 16c1–16c5 ratings and must be read with the complete rationale.
