Research examplesUS 9,331,489 B2

Electrical engineering · Power electronics

Trace the power path when sunlight falls.

Multi-input boost converters, combined DC output and the electronics supply at low power.

CyboEnergyUS 9,331,489 B2 · Claim 14Submitted research

The technical question

How do earlier multi-input inverter designs supply their own electronics, particularly when available input power is low?

The research approach

The research traced the connections among boost stages, DC links and on-board supply rails. Each part of the difficult auxiliary-supply limitation is mapped independently.

Connections made inspectable

Follow the electrical path and distinguish what is stated from what still needs support.

Ground 1 · Obviousness over JP 2001-255949 A (Morishima) in view of WO 2011/107555 A1 (Müller) and US 7,796,412 B2 (Fornage)

References

JP 2001-255949 A (Morishima, Toshiba) · WO 2011/107555 A1 (Müller, SMA Solar Technology) · US 7,796,412 B2 (Fornage, Enphase Energy)

Recorded verdict

Rated D, hold 20, in the re-score of 18 October 2026: the alternative to the submitted Ground 2. Limitations 1pre to 14d are rated as in Ground 2; 14e falls to D 25 because, without Suekane, the switch of the supply onto the dedicated string rests on the broad reading that claim 14 states only the condition under which the supply takes that string’s power. Morishima’s diodes feed the supply from the highest string at low light whatever the controller detects, and the owner’s reply that nothing changes on the detection is strong.

1pre to 14d

The inverter, its input channels, AC output, combiner and microcontroller, as in the submitted chart.

“The present invention relates to a photovoltaic power generation inverter device that performs a DC-AC power conversion operation by using a plurality of solar cell assemblies as inputs.”

Morishima · JP 2001-255949 A · [0001] · English print p. 1

Fornage’s power threshold gives 14d its low-power mode, as in Ground 2.

14e

A DC power supply connected to all input channels through the DC power combiner, supplying the inverter’s electronic circuits, and configured to take DC power from a dedicated input channel and its solar panel when the microcontroller detects that the calculated input power is below a pre-determined value.

“The on-board supply unit 14 takes electric energy both out of the DC voltage link 10 and the DC voltage link 6.”

Müller · WO 2011/107555 A1 · p. 10, lines 3–4

The supply fed from the common link as well as the input links, added to Morishima’s supply.

14e

The dedicated channel under this ground.

“Out of which the solar cell string outputting the highest voltage is operated.”

Morishima · [0072] · English print p. 6

The diodes give the supply the highest string at low light without any act of the controller: the open point of this ground.

14e

The pre-determined value.

“At step 604, the method 600 performs calculations and checks if the input power is greater than a power threshold.”

Fornage · US 7,796,412 B2 · Column 6, lines 55–58

Ground 3 · Ground 2 further in view of JP H01-318519 A (Yoshimi), for the reading of “dedicated” as set apart from generation

References

JP 2001-255949 A (Morishima, Toshiba) · US 7,796,412 B2 (Fornage, Enphase Energy) · US 2011/0169344 A1 (Suekane) · WO 2011/107555 A1 (Müller, SMA Solar Technology) · JP H01-318519 A (Yoshimi)

Recorded verdict

Rated D, hold 34, in the re-score of 18 October 2026: the further alternative. Rows 1pre to 14d are as in Ground 2, and 14e is D 42: under a reading of “dedicated” as set apart from generation, Yoshimi’s string, set apart by its switch, rises to near its open-circuit voltage and alone carries the control supply while the other strings keep generating, applied by stopping one string’s chopper in the low-power mode. The reason is inferred and it is a fifth reference, with the owner’s wasted-output answer and Yoshimi’s age and field colourable.
