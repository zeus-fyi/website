Research examplesUS 9,900,067 B2

Electrical engineering · Communications

A bandwidth-dependent field deserves a deeper reading.

Control-field ordering and the number of bits used for a data-length indicator.

Sol IPUS 9,900,067 B2 · Claim 38Submitted research

The technical question

What earlier material connects channel bandwidth to the size of a control field while preserving the claimed packet structure?

The research approach

The search combined WLAN patent disclosures, conference literature and telecommunications standards. It separated evidence usable under the contest date from a labelled alternative dependent on another priority-date reading.

Dates kept explicit

Keep the operative evidence separate from alternatives that depend on a different date.

Limitation 38h · C 52 · Weakest

Fewer length bits at 40 MHz than at a wider band.

No document before the contest date gives one WLAN length field fewer bits at 40 MHz than above it. NEC’s LTE-Advanced proposal evaluates the sizing rule at the claim’s own bandwidths, 15 bits at 40 MHz against 16, 17 and 18 above it; Nokia Siemens and 3GPP state the rule in WiMAX and UTRA control fields; Syafei gives the WLAN reason and the 802.11 standard its own precedent.

Passages on the original page

the size of the burst length field 404 may be selected by determining the frequency or bandwidth used for communication with the MS.

a minimum of 8 bits is needed to represent all possible available 192 resource blocks (a savings of 2 bits compared to the 802.16e 10 bit reference).

What the score reflects

The weakest limitation, a coin flip: an obviousness argument across standards. The rule references outside WLAN meet the owner’s analogous-art replies, the WLAN art of the time kept the field fixed, and the step is an inferred multi-reference combination.

Claim-wide point P1 · B 88

The reason to build Airgo’s 40–100 MHz packets on the 802.11n transceivers and second-signal-field layouts of Broadcom and Sony, and with Syafei’s two signal fields, is stated in the art of one field: Airgo names its second field an “11n Signal Field”, Syafei keeps the HT-SIG for backward compatibility with 802.11n, and all are dated before the contest date.

Limitations 38a, 38b and 38d rest on this point; the owner’s answer is hindsight.

Claim-wide point P2 · C 70

Read by its plain meaning, after rather than immediately after: claim 38 does not say “immediately”, and the applicant wrote “in order” and “consists of” where it wanted sequence and size. Under “immediately subsequent” every layout before the date fails, with a last-PSDU bit between the length and the reserved bits and the CRC between the reserved bits and the tail.

Limitations 38f and 38g rest on this point. The owner’s reading is colourable: the patent’s own field puts length, reserved and tail bits side by side because it moves the CRC to the service field, a choice claim 38 does not recite.
