Research examplesUS 10,090,894 B2

Electrical engineering · Communications

The service field, examined bit by bit.

Frame order, scrambler bits and a CRC carried in the service field.

Sol IPUS 10,090,894 B2 · Claim 7Submitted research

The technical question

Which earlier disclosures place the relevant frame elements in order and carry both scrambler-related bits and a CRC in the service field?

The research approach

The chart connects earlier WLAN signaling formats with a service-field integrity check. A separately stated multi-user alternative addresses another reading of the signal fields.

Precise technical mapping

Inspect the exact field, bit allocation and source location behind a proposed mapping.

Source excerpt

US 7,231,530 B1

Column 9, lines 41–44 · PDF page 15

“the integrity of at least one bit of the PLCP header, e.g., the SIGNAL field, is verified using a polynomial (CRC) check sequence located in at least one bit of the SERVICE field.”
Read the reference

The mapping

The passage is charted for limitation 7f2. Further passages identify scrambler-initialization bits and unused bits in the service field; other references carry the frame structure.

Scope of this excerpt

The submission proposes a combination. The CRC passage is not presented as a single-reference disclosure of every limitation, and the service-field issue remains the chart’s weakest element.
