Website and inquiry data

This site does not include advertising pixels, analytics scripts or account registration. Its inquiry form prepares an email locally in your browser. Form contents are not transmitted to a site backend. Choosing “Open email draft” opens your email application; you decide whether to send the message.

The hosting provider may process routine connection information when serving pages. If you send an email, Zeusfyi receives the information in that message and uses it to respond and discuss the proposed engagement.

Confidential material

Use public information in an initial inquiry. Agree how confidential materials will be handled before sharing them. Any engagement-specific confidentiality, deliverable, fee and timing terms belong in the written agreement.

Research examples

Published examples are excerpts of submitted research. They do not imply that a named patent owner is a client, that a contest prize was awarded or that a patent has been invalidated. Quotations, summaries and researcher inferences are identified separately.

Research assessments

Scores describe an internal assessment of a particular mapping or proposed ground. They are not calibrated outcome probabilities. Zeusfyi provides technical research and documentation; counsel determines how to use the work.
